## Words and phrases

**gate** — The place where you go to get on your plane.

**flight-information screen** — A screen with information about flights, gates and times.

**boarding pass** — The document you need before you can get on the plane.

**departure board** — The screen with your leaving flight on it.

**boarding** — Getting on the plane.

**flight number** — The letters and numbers used to identify your flight.

**hallway** — A long space inside a building that you walk through.

**passengers** — People who are traveling.

**whether** — Used when something may be true or may not be true.

**I think my gate changed.** — I believe my flight now leaves from a different gate.

**Which gate is it now?** — Tell me the new gate.

**How do I get there from here?** — Tell me the way from this place to that place.

**Has boarding started yet?** — Are passengers getting on the plane now?

**Not yet.** — It has not happened, but it may happen later.
