## Words and phrases

**espresso machine** — A machine that makes strong coffee.

**pastry case** — A glass box in a café that shows cakes or other baked food.

**work partner** — A person who works with you on the same job.

**client** — A person who asks a business to do work and pays for it.

**design plan** — A plan that shows how something should look or work.

**customer display** — A small screen that shows a customer the order.

**grinder** — A machine that breaks coffee beans into very small pieces.

**pickup side** — The place where you wait for and collect your order.

**receipt** — A paper that shows what you bought and paid for.

**order ticket** — A paper or screen note that tells a worker what to make.

**dairy milk** — Milk that comes from a cow or another animal.

**carton** — A light box used to hold milk or juice.

**steam wand** — A thin part of a coffee machine that heats milk.

**remake** — To make something again.

**corrected** — Changed so it is right.

**underway** — Happening now after it has started.

**appreciate** — To be thankful for something.

**holder** — A tray that helps you carry cups.

**confirm** — To say that something is correct.

**spill** — To make liquid fall out of a cup by accident.

**Can I have a coffee with oat milk, please?** — A friendly way to ask for this drink.

**Sorry, what was that?** — Please say that again because I did not hear it.

**For here or to go?** — Will you drink it here, or take it away?

**Anything else?** — Do you want another item?

**Could I also have ...?** — A polite way to add another item.

**Excuse me.** — A polite way to get someone's attention.

**Could you make it again with oat milk, please?** — Please replace the wrong drink with one made using oat milk.

**Is this the one with oat milk?** — Please confirm which drink has oat milk.
